Privacy · effective September 20, 2026

What we collect,
in plain English.

Short version: this site uses no cookies and no third‑party trackers. We count page views and link clicks ourselves, without storing anything that identifies you. If you send us a form, we use it to reply to you. That's it.

Who we are

GroundWork‑Web, Deer Park, Texas. We build websites for auto detailers. Questions about this policy: email us.

Site analytics (no cookies)

To understand which pages and links are useful, our own server records:

  • The page you viewed (the path only — never the full address, so nothing typed into a form or link ends up in our logs)
  • Links you click and the text of the link
  • Whether you submitted the audit or start form (not what you typed)
  • The site that sent you here, if any (domain name only)
  • The campaign tag on the link you arrived from, if we sent it to you (e.g. "email")
  • Screen size class — phone, tablet or desktop

To count unique visitors, we combine your IP address and browser type with a secret that changes every day, and store only a short hash of the result. We never store your IP address or browser details, and the hash cannot be turned back into them. After 24 hours it is useless for recognizing anyone — which is the point.

We honor the Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track browser signals: if either is on, we record nothing. We do not set analytics cookies, use fingerprinting, or share this data with anyone. Analytics records are deleted after 13 months.

Forms you send us

If you request an audit or start a build, we collect what you enter — typically your shop name, city, name, email, phone, and notes — and use it to reply to you and deliver what you asked for. Your browser also keeps a copy of your entries locally (so nothing is lost if you leave and come back) and remembers which link brought you to us so we can reference the right demo. Neither leaves your device except as part of the form you submit.

We keep lead information for as long as we are talking or working together, and delete it on request.

Payments and scheduling

Checkout is handled by Stripe; we never see your full card number. Audit calls are scheduled through a booking provider (such as Calendly). Those services have their own privacy policies which apply when you use them.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or social‑media pixels
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your information
  • No cross‑site tracking
  • No cookies for analytics

Your choices

  • Turn on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser and we record nothing.
  • Email us to see, correct, or delete anything you've sent us. We'll respond within 30 days.

Email we send

If you receive a business email from us, it will say who we are, include a real mailing address, and let you opt out with one reply. Ask once and we stop.

Changes

If we change how this site handles data — for example, by adding a third‑party tool — we'll update this page and the effective date above, and add a consent notice where one is required.
