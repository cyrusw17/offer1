Fine print, plainly

Terms

How much does a detailing website cost elsewhere?

Niche agencies quote $1,500–$5,000 up front plus $150–$400 a month, over 4–8 weeks. DIY builders run $20–$50 a month and cost you the weekends, and most end up looking homemade. We're $99 today and $399 total, then $99 or $199 a month, live in about a week, because we only build detailing sites and we'd rather earn the monthly plan than the build fee.

Is the booking system extra?

No. Embedding your booking tool — Square Appointments, Booksy, Google, Urable or what you already use — is part of the $399 build. A fully custom booking engine is the only thing quoted separately, and most shops don't need one.

How do I pay?

Secure checkout via Stripe. $99 today to start the build. $300 when you approve the preview. Host or Grow bills the same card starting the month your site goes live.

What if I don't like the site?

You get one revision on a private preview. If you don't want the site, we refund the $99. The $300 is charged only when you approve it live. After that, you can leave with 30 days notice.

Can I cancel?

Yes — 30 days written notice, no cancellation fee. After your final invoice we hand over your site files.

Will the price go up on me?

No. Founding clients keep their rate for as long as they stay. New clients after the founding wave pay more.

Do you do ads?

Not right now. We'll get you found organically — Google Business Profile, on‑page SEO, and a site people actually book on.
