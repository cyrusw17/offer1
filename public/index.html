Questions

Honest answers

How much does a car detailing website cost?

With us: $99 today, then $300 when you approve the preview, $399 total. Host is $99 a month or Grow is $199 a month, starting the month you go live. If you don't want the site after the revision, we refund the $99. Booking, prices, gallery, reviews, and on-page SEO are in the build. Agencies quote $1,500–$5,000 for the same site.

Do I need a website if I already have Instagram or Facebook?

Instagram is a portfolio, not a storefront. Google doesn't rank your posts for "car detailing near me," and nobody can see prices or grab a time in a DM. The site is what turns a search into a booked appointment — and it's what your Google Business Profile links to.

Can customers book online?

Yes. We embed the booking tool that fits you — Square Appointments, Booksy, Google, Urable, or what you already use — so customers pick a package and a time on your site without texting you. Custom booking is available later as a quoted add‑on.

Do you work with mobile detailers?

Mobile detailers get a service‑area layout — the cities and zips you drive to, a we‑come‑to‑you booking flow — and a Google Business Profile set up as a service‑area business so you rank across your territory, not just your home address.

Do I have to be in Texas?

No. We're based in Deer Park, Texas. The sample shops are fictional and set in real US cities. The build, booking, and monthly plans are open to detailers anywhere in the US. Tell us your city and we start there.

How do I show up for "detailing near me" in my city?

Three things decide it: a complete Google Business Profile with the right category, real photos and steady reviews; a fast site that names your services and cities and shows prices; and matching business details everywhere. Every build handles the site part and gives you a checklist for the profile part. Read the full checklist →

How long does it take?

About 7 days from the day we have your logo, 10–20 photos, services and prices. Private preview, one revision round, nothing goes live until you approve. No photos yet? We launch with a clean layout and swap them in later.

Do I have to get on a call?

No. Start now is the $99 checkout and we go straight to work. Book the audit if you want a free 15-minute call first. You see the site before the $300. If you don't want it, the $99 comes back.

Who owns the domain and the site?

You own your domain. We register it in your name when you approve the site. The site stays live on Host or Grow. If you cancel, we hand over the files after the final invoice.

What counts as an "update" on Grow?

Small edits under ~45 minutes each: a new promo banner, price change, new service blurb, gallery additions, seasonal CTA. A brand‑new page or full redesign is quoted separately.
